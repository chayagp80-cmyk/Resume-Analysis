Data Analyst

We are seeking a Data Analyst to support business decisions through analysis, reporting, and dashboarding.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze large datasets using SQL and Python.
- Build dashboards and reports for stakeholders.
- Communicate trends and recommendations clearly.
- Work with cross-functional teams to define KPIs and measure performance.
- Support data quality initiatives and process improvement.

Requirements:
- 3+ years of experience in data analysis or business intelligence.
- Strong SQL skills and experience with Python.
- Experience with BI tools such as Power BI or Tableau.
- Excellent communication and presentation skills.

Preferred:
- Experience in retail or e-commerce.
- Knowledge of ETL pipelines and data modeling.
